🧠 PART E — Full Explanation of Your Heuristic Steganography Algorithms
(This is exactly what you should say in viva)
Your project implements multi-domain heuristic steganalysis across images, PDFs, and documents.
Below is the clear, polished version:

1) LSB (Least Significant Bit) Analysis
Used for PNG, BMP, uncompressed images.
Idea:
Natural images have random noise in the LSB layer.
Steganography modifies LSBs to hide data.
Your detectors:


LSB suspicious_score → measures imbalance of 0/1 distribution in LSBs.


Chi-square test → detects statistical artifacts caused by embedding.


Entropy test → checks randomness within LSB plane.


High deviation = possible LSB stego.

2) JPEG DCT Analysis
JPEG stores data in DCT coefficients block-by-block.
Stego methods like F5, JSteg, OutGuess modify DCT coefficients.
Your detectors:


DCT coefficient histogram flatness


Hidden data reduces the natural uneven distribution.




Parity analysis


JSteg modifies even/odd patterns.




dct_suspicion score



3) File Append Detector (Polyglot Stego)
Many beginner stego tools attach payload after FFD9 (JPEG EOF).
Your detector:


Scans file tail for hidden content


Checks entropy of tail region


Detects magic bytes like PK, ZIP, RAR, PNG, etc.



4) PNG Chunk Steganography Detector
PNG has structured chunks. Steganographers use:


custom chunks


iTXt/zTXt compressed text


extra metadata with hidden payload


Your detector:


Enumerates all chunks


Flags any non-standard or suspicious chunk


Measures chunk entropy


Searches chunk payload for compressed data or ZIP headers



5) IDAT Steganography (Advanced)
The Kaggle dataset hides payload inside zlib-compressed IDAT.
Your forensic pipeline:


Combine all IDAT chunks


Zlib-decompress into raw scanline bytes


Search for:


ZIP signatures


EOCD


High-entropy blocks




Carve raw binary blobs


Save .idat.raw and .carved.bin for analysis


This is an advanced forensic technique.

6) PDF Steganography Detection
Analyzes PDF object streams for:


High entropy


Embedded files


Suspicious JS


Binary streams with ZIP signatures



7) DOCX Steganography Detection
DOCX is simply a ZIP with:


word/media/*.png


word/embeddings/*


vbaProject.bin


Your detector extracts all media & embedded objects and scans them with the image pipeline.
